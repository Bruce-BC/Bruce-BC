## 🧬 About Me

Portfolio, publications and full CV: [bruce-bc.github.io](https://bruce-bc.github.io)

* Senior Researcher, Bioinformatics at **MicrobiotiX**: *Klebsiella* phage genomics, tail/RBP and capsule K-type analysis, and structure-guided design of host-recognition proteins.
* 6+ years in industry. Led the bioinformatics team at **Sanigen** (2020–2025), running bacterial, phage and metagenome NGS analysis services.
* Built a citation-traceable database of **7,759** *Klebsiella* phage depolymerase and tail-protein sequences, with K-type calls calibrated by identity bin (95–100%: **97.1%** accuracy).
* Unified 16S, ITS, WGS and Illumina–ONT hybrid analysis under one Snakemake entry point with containerized environments.
* Single-cell RNA-seq: built a colorectal cancer single-cell atlas and annotated its cell types (Wittgenbio, 2026).
* **7** SCI(E) papers and **1** patent, including NGS multiplex panels for foodborne-pathogen detection.
